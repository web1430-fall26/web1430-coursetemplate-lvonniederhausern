About Us!

My fictional product is painted rocks sold as paperweights for desks and other surfaces. Sold to people who are at their desk for long periods of time and want something to make it feel a bit more homey. It will also add some much-needed decoration and a burst of color to otherwise paper-covered desks. They are hand selected and painted by me. They come in a large variety of colors and designs, no two rocks are the same! They add the fun to functional.

Accordion Menu: Frequently Asked Questions

I find it a very fun and relaxing activity. I love to paint and prefer to paint iregular objects. I feel like it gives my art more personality and character.

There are many different designs and colors. They can be anything from a landscape to a motivational message. There are no limits to the design, and I have access to every color in the rainbow.

Rocks come in small, medium, and large sizes. Small rocks are smaller than your palm, Medium rocks are about the size of your palm, and large rocks are larger than your fist.

Pricing is based on the amount of resources used, when it was made, and how long it took to make. Rocks with more intricate designs, including text or a specific image, tend to cost more.
